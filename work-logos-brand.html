Forever Young

TY Young, WNBA player · Logo and lettering

Goal
Introduce her Forever Young line of TY1 Gear apparel
Delivered
Forever Young logo, black and white vector
Budget
$300 – $500 (logo mark only)

The brief

TY Young, a WNBA star, came to me by referral from a previous client. She was launching Forever Young, a new line of her TY1 Gear apparel, and needed a hand-lettered logo with real graffiti attitude to carry it.

How it was made

It started as a pencil sketch on notebook paper, working out the letter shapes, the shadow and a checkerboard pattern. From there I redrew it as clean black and white artwork that holds up on a phone screen, on apparel and on a banner. The sketch's checkerboard became the shadow in a full-color version.

How it went

TY introduced the logo herself, holding it up on her phone in an Instagram post for her Breakfast with Tamera series, tagged #TY1Gear and #ForeverYoung. The post drew over 2,000 likes.

Artist Couture

Angel Merino (@macdaddy), makeup artist · Logo and metallic graphics

Goal
A logo for his new makeup line, made to sell in Sephora worldwide
Delivered
Gold metallic logo for light and dark backgrounds
Budget
$450 – $650

The brief

Angel Merino used to be Beyoncé's makeup artist in LA. He found my metallic graphic work on Instagram and came to me for his new makeup line. He wanted a logo that looked like Illuminati mixed with Versace.

How it was made

I took the engraved, all-seeing-eye look of the Illuminati reference and the gold baroque scrollwork of the Versace-style reference and combined them into one ornate letter A. I then worked the metal finish until it read as real gold, with a version for white backgrounds and a version for black.

How it went

The logo launched on the packaging for the Artist Couture line, in gold on the black lid and box. Later the company dropped the detailed graphic from the top of the engraving, because engraving that much detail was too difficult to produce. They now use only the typography.

In use

Blalock's

Blalock's Professional Beauty College · Logo

Goal
A new logo to rebrand the college
Delivered
Logo in sky-blue with a woman's profile built into the B
Budget
$350 – $550 (logo only)

The brief

The owner's daughter commissioned the logo. After the owner passed away, the college was rebranded with it.

How it was made

The B is the center of the mark. A woman's profile and flowing hair are cut into the letter, so the logo says "beauty college" before anyone reads the name. The letters use a glossy sky-blue gradient, and the college name sits underneath in clean black type.

How it went

The sleek new logo is now the face of the college. It lights up the storefront sign at night and fills the glass entrance door as vinyl, with the college name curving around the B. The round version of the mark is also printed in gold on royal blue "Beauties" t-shirts.
